# Celo-HaiTi Governance

Celo-HaiTi's Governance Council is its highest ongoing collective decision-making body. Governance authority is collective and is not held unilaterally by the Founder, a Foundation Director, a Maintainer, a Working Group, or any other individual role.

## Role Boundaries

- **Governance Council:** Holds the highest ongoing collective decision-making authority for Celo-HaiTi's governance.
- **Founder:** Johnny Dubic is permanently recognized as the Founder in Celo-HaiTi's historical and institutional record. Founder status does not confer governance authority, ownership rights, veto power, or unilateral control.
- **Foundation Director:** Any founding, representative, strategic, or operational responsibilities assigned to this role operate within the governance framework. The role is not above, and cannot unilaterally override, the Governance Council.
- **Maintainers:** Provide technical and project stewardship within their documented scope. Maintenance responsibility is not governance authority.
- **Working Groups:** Carry out delegated or scoped work within their documented mandates. A Working Group does not become a higher governance body by performing operational work.
- **Contributors:** Participate by proposing, building, reviewing, and supporting Celo-HaiTi work according to the contribution and governance framework.

Operational, technical, partnership, and representation responsibilities must not be presented as governance authority. This document does not create additional governance mechanisms, approval requirements, membership rules, voting procedures, or individual powers.
